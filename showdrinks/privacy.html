Privacy Policy

Pirton Players ShowDrinks

We collect only what's needed to take and hand over your order: your name, the items you order, and — if you pay online — confirmation of payment from SumUp.

Payment card details are handled entirely by SumUp under their own privacy policy — they never reach our systems.

Order data is kept for the run of the show for record-keeping, and deleted after a reasonable period afterward.

We don't sell or share your information with any third party beyond SumUp (for processing payment) and our own show staff (for preparing your order).

Questions, or want your data deleted sooner? Email web@pirtonplayers.org.
