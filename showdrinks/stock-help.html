Stock system help

Counting stock, recording deliveries, splitting stock between organisations, and tracking every movement.

1. The basics

Open the admin page and choose Stock in the main menu. Everything for stock lives on this one page. The three views are switched with the footer at the bottom of the screen (Stock list, Stock movements, Restock); the ⋯ button at the top right holds the stock actions.

ViewWhat it is for
Stock listThe item list: set cost, minimum and restock level, add or remove stock, and count stock.
Stock movementsA history of every stock take, restock and clear, by organisation and by item.
RestockThe list of what to order, based on what is on hand.

Only items with Stk ticked on the Price List item screen are part of stock. Use Show non-stock items to see the rest.

2. The Stock list

Items are grouped by category. Tap a category button to filter, or All.

ColumnMeaning
Cost £What one unit costs you. Every stock item needs a cost above 0, or Zero cost ticked on the item's edit screen (pencil) for items that really are free. Until then stock takes, opening stock, restocks and the Add button are blocked and the items missing a cost are listed. Stock is valued at the cost it had when it was added. Changing it later does not change the value of stock you already hold.
MinThe level at or below which the item should be reordered.
Restock levelWhat a restock tops the item up to. Blank means "same as Min". It can be higher than Min, never lower.
On handThe quantity you hold now: the last count plus anything added since.
+/− buttonAdd or remove stock outside a count (see section 4).
CountedOnly used during a stock take (see section 3).

Tick Show non-zero only to hide items with nothing on hand. It is also available during a stock take; an item you have already entered a count for stays visible.

Stock take counts can be whole numbers or, for items such as bottles, quarters (¼ ½ ¾) in one box that + and − move by a quarter. Restock and Add quantities are always whole units.

3. Stock takes

A stock take counts everything and works out what was used since the last count.

Start a stock take

  1. Open the ⋯ menu and choose Start stock take.
  2. Type an event name (for example Autumn show). Pick which organisation the take is for.
  3. The page title changes to Stock take in progress, and the footer item reads Resume stock take until you finish or cancel.

Count

  • Enter the number you actually have in the Counted column, using − [n] + or typing. Fractions are available for items sold in quarters.
  • Your counts are saved on this device as a draft, so you can leave the page and come back with Resume stock take.
  • An item you leave empty counts as 0.

Finish

  1. Open ⋯ → Finish stock take.
  2. Check the results: for each item, the opening figure, anything added, what you counted, what was used, and the cost of what was used. Used items only is ticked by default; untick it to see every item.
  3. Press Confirm to save. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

Finishing sets each item's new starting figure to your count. Usage is charged to the organisation the take was for.

Cancel

⋯ → Cancel stock take throws away the count in progress and changes nothing.

Opening stock

Use ⋯ → Set opening stock for an organisation's first count. It is only allowed for an organisation that holds no stock; after that use stock takes and restocks. Pick the organisation, count, and finish. It sets starting figures but records no usage.

Delete a mistake

Only the latest entry can be deleted: the newest stock take, opening stock, clear or restock. Open it from Stock movements and press Delete. Deleting a stock take puts every item back as it was before; deleting a restock takes its quantities back out of stock. Then the next-newest entry can be deleted, and so on.

4. Adding and removing stock between counts

Use the +/− button on an item line for a delivery, top-up or write-off.

  1. Press the button on the item.
  2. Use + or − to enter the quantity.
  3. Pick the organisation it is for.
  4. Press OK.

Nothing is written until you press OK. It is recorded as a restock (or a removal if negative), not as usage, so it does not count as sales.

5. Restock

The Restock view lists what to order, worked out from current stock against each item's Min and Restock level.

  • Order column: edit a quantity by hand if you want something different. The bin icon removes an item from the list.
  • + Add item: add an item that is not on the list.
  • Recalculate: rebuild the list from current stock. Quantities you set by hand are replaced, and items you removed come back if they need ordering.
  • Print: a printable order list with estimated cost.
  • Add restock to stock: use this when the delivery arrives. Choose the organisation it is for. It adds everything on the list to stock as a delivery and clears the list. This cannot be undone from here.

6. Organisations

Organisations (for example Pirton Players or Film Club) each hold a share of the stock. Open Organisations in the Bar Manager menu to add, rename or delete them. An organisation that still holds stock, or owes some, cannot be deleted.

Organisations are mandatory, so:

  • Every stock take, opening stock count, delivery and +/− must say which organisation it is for.
  • A take charges usage to its organisation. If the organisation used more than its share, it shows as owed in red.
  • Stock not held by any organisation shows as Unassigned.

7. Stock movements

Open Stock movements in the footer. There are two tabs.

Organisations tab

One line per organisation, plus Unassigned if there is any, and a bold Total stock line, each with the value of stock held (at cost; red means owed).

  • Tap a line to slide open its history, newest first. Tap again to close.
  • Tap a history line to open the details: a stock take opens its report, a restock opens the list of items in it.
  • Long histories are split into pages: use ‹ Newer and Older ›.
  • The Total history includes an Organisation column; an organisation's own history does not need it.

Opening stock, usage, restocks and "stock cleared" appear in date order with a running Balance.

Items tab

Every item that has moved, grouped by category, with the quantity held now. Tap an item to see its history: date, organisation, event, item, price, movement and running quantity balance. Stock takes show as used n or counted n; restocks as +n; stock cleared as reset to 0.

On a phone, dates show as dd/mm/yy.

How stock is valued

Stock is valued at the cost it had when it was added. A price rise only affects stock added after the rise. Usage uses up the oldest stock first.

"Other adjustments (not itemised)"

If the history cannot fully explain the quantity held now, for example stock changed before restocks were logged, one row shows the difference. Tap it to see which items disagree: the quantity according to the history, the quantity held now, and the value difference.

Print and email

The restock detail window has Print and Email buttons. Email opens your mail program with the details filled in.

8. Other actions

9. Quick answers

A delivery arrived. What do I do? Restock → Add restock to stock (if it was on the list), or use +/− on each item.

I made a stock take or restock by mistake. If it is the latest entry, open it and delete it. Otherwise cancel before finishing.

An organisation shows as owed. It used more stock than it held. A restock or opening stock for that organisation will bring it back up.

I changed a cost price. Did my stock value change? No. Only stock added after the change uses the new price.

There is an Other adjustments row I do not understand. Tap it. It lists each item whose quantity does not match the history.
