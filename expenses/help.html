Help

Pirton Players Expenses

Signing in

A manager sets up your account and gives you a temporary password. The first time you sign in with it, you'll be asked to set your own — you can't do anything else until that's done.

Forgotten your password? Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen — you'll get an emailed link to choose a new one.

Adding an expense

Pick the show, describe what it was for, enter the amount, and attach photo(s) of the receipt — take a new photo or choose existing ones from your library, and attach as many as you need. At least one receipt photo is required.

Tap + Add to list to stage it, then fill in the form again for the next one. Nothing is actually submitted yet — build up as many as you like first.

Before you submit

Each item on the Ready to submit list has two icons: the pencil lets you edit it (change any field, remove a photo, or add more — new photos add to what's already there rather than replacing it), and the bin removes it from the list entirely.

When everything's ready, tap Submit all — every item on the list is sent in one go, and the managers get a single email listing all of them, not one email each.

Expenses list

Your unpaid total is shown at the top of the New expense screen. Switch to Expenses list (menu, top-left) to see the full list — toggle between Unpaid only and All.

Tap any expense to see its full detail, including the receipt photo(s) you attached.

Getting paid

Once a manager marks an expense as paid, you'll get an email confirming it — there's nothing else you need to do.
